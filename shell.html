On this suite

Eight plates, drawn for the screen rather than the stone. Every stair, tile, tribar and disc here is constructed in markup — original geometry in Escher's grammar, computed rather than reproduced. No print of his is copied or embedded; his actual work remains with the M.C. Escher heritage.

Maurits Cornelis Escher was born in Leeuwarden in 1898 and died in 1972. He wanted to be an architect, and instead learned woodcut, etching and linocut under Samuel Jessurun de Mesquita in Haarlem until 1922. The stairs came from Penrose; the hyperbolic disc came from a diagram sent by the geometer H.S.M. Coxeter. He was, as one curator put it, loved by the mathematicians and the hippies alike.
